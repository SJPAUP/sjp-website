Social Voices

Journalists & On-the-Ground Reporters

  • Plestia Alaqad – Gaza-based citizen journalist sharing first-hand videos of life and conflict in Gaza.
  • Motaz Azaiza – Palestinian photojournalist documenting frontline events and daily life.
  • Wael Al-Dahdouh – Veteran Palestinian journalist, bureau chief of Al Jazeera in Gaza.
  • Hind Khoudary – Gaza-based journalist reporting on the humanitarian crisis and conflict.
  • Yousef Hammash – Palestinian freelance journalist covering Gaza and occupied territories.
  • Bisan Owda – Award-winning journalist, activist, and filmmaker documenting Gaza.

Creativity Amidst The Genocide

  • Mohammed and Omar Show – Young content creator from Gaza sharing daily life, humor, and community-focused videos that highlight resilience and youth culture.
  • Ahmed Abu Amsha – Palestinian musician from Gaza known for performing on the oud and transforming drone and missile sounds into music.
  • Renad Attalah – A young Palestinian culinary creator, and food storyteller sharing local cuisine and cultural recipes.
