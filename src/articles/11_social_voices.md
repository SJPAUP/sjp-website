### Journalists & On-the-Ground Reporters

- **[Plestia Alaqad](https://www.instagram.com/plestia.alaqad/)** – Gaza-based citizen journalist sharing first-hand videos of life and conflict in Gaza.  
- **[Motaz Azaiza](https://www.instagram.com/motaz_azaiza)** – Palestinian photojournalist documenting frontline events and daily life.  
- **[Wael Al-Dahdouh](https://www.instagram.com/wael_eldahdouh)** – Veteran Palestinian journalist, bureau chief of Al Jazeera in Gaza.  
- **[Hind Khoudary](https://www.instagram.com/hindkhoudary)** – Gaza-based journalist reporting on the humanitarian crisis and conflict.  
- **[Yousef Hammash](https://www.instagram.com/alijadallah66)** – Palestinian freelance journalist covering Gaza and occupied territories.  
- **[Bisan Owda](https://www.instagram.com/wizard_bisan1)** – Award-winning journalist, activist, and filmmaker documenting Gaza.

### Creativity Amidst The Genocide   

- **[Mohammed and Omar Show](https://www.instagram.com/omarherzshow/?hl=en)** – Young content creator from Gaza sharing daily life, humor, and community-focused videos that highlight resilience and youth culture.   
- **[Ahmed Abu Amsha](https://www.instagram.com/ahmedmuin_abuamsha/)** – Palestinian musician from Gaza known for performing on the oud and transforming drone and missile sounds into music.
- **[Renad Attalah](https://www.instagram.com/renadattalah)** – A young Palestinian culinary creator, and food storyteller sharing local cuisine and cultural recipes.
